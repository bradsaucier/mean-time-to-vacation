Meet the island below the surface.

Every reef has its own rhythm. Start shallow, drift along a wall, or follow a local guide into clear blue water.

Easygoing introduction

Gale Reef

A bright, sheltered site with gentle conditions and long sightlines. Gale Reef is a comfortable first look at the island's coral gardens.

Best for: new divers and relaxed snorkel sessions

Color in motion

Dawson's Reef

Follow the current past sea fans and small schools of reef fish. Dawson's rewards a patient pace and a sharp eye.

Best for: confident swimmers and underwater photography

The deeper horizon

Claire's Reef

A dramatic outer-reef profile with cooler water and a sweeping blue backdrop. Local guides tailor the route to conditions each morning.

Best for: experienced divers with a guide
